READING
------------------------------------------------------------
The threshold is 62.9 times higher at the D1 calibration than at Coles-Kelishomi's
(12.78 vs 0.203), because delta_e = tau makes the standing vacancy stock 83% new
postings, leaving only 17% inherited for a destruction shock to remove. At the D1
value 93% of the stock is inherited.

delta_e = tau is the maximum admissible destruction rate (s >= 0), so CK sit at the
point that MINIMIZES the entry threshold over the whole admissible range.
