\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item $\bar{g}^O$ ($\bar{g}^C$): fraction of gross job gains (losses) from opening (closing) establishments $= \sum G_O / \sum G$.
\item $\sigma^O/\sigma$: std dev of HP-filtered gains at openings relative to std dev of HP-filtered total gains (col~3 in JF); filter applied to raw levels. $\sigma^C/\sigma$: same for losses.
\item $\mathcal{V}^O/\mathcal{V}$: ratio of fitted variances from projecting HP-filtered flows on HP-filtered unemployment ($u_t$, $u_{t-1}$); JF use detrended GDP.
\item Values $>1$: entry/exit margin more cyclically sensitive than total flows.
\item $^\dagger$ Noisy ($|\text{ratio}|>5$; small-count series).
