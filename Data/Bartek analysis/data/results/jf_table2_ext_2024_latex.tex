\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item \textit{Mean share}: fraction of gross gains (losses) from opening (closing) establishments.
\item \textit{Cycl.\ var.}: $R^2$ from regressing HP- (or Hamilton-) filtered log total flows on filtered log entry/exit flows.
\item \textit{Unemp.\ proj.}: ratio of fitted variances from projecting log series on constant $+u_t+u_{t-1}$ (national unemployment rate).
\item Values $>1$ in unemp.\ columns indicate entry/exit is more cyclically sensitive than total flows.
