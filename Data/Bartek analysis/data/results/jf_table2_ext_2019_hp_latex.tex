\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item $\bar{g}^O$ ($\bar{g}^C$): fraction of gross job gains (losses) from opening (closing) establishments $= \sum G_O / \sum G$.
\item $\sigma^O/\sigma$: std dev of filtered gains at openings relative to std dev of filtered total gains (col~3 in JF); filter applied to raw levels. $\sigma^C/\sigma$: same for losses.
\item $R^2_O$ ($R^2_C$): $R^2$ from regressing $\widetilde{\log u_t}$ on $\widetilde{\log G_{O,t}}$ ($\widetilde{\log L_{C,t}}$), where $\widetilde{\cdot}$ denotes the filtered log cycle. Both series log-transformed before filtering (unit-comparable log-deviations). Fraction of unemployment-cycle variance explained by the entry/exit margin.
\item Values near 0: extensive margin contributes little to unemployment cycles (intensive margin dominates). Values near 1: extensive margin tracks the cycle.
\item $^\dagger$ Noisy ($|\text{ratio}|>5$; small-count series).
\end{tablenotes}
